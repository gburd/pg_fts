# pg_fts 1.15.2 release record (2026-11-03)

**Current.**  1.15.2 = 1.15.1 + two fixes for ranked queries:
- the tombstone-lookup fix (ROADMAP I25, 8079c31);
- the block-max WAND pivot fix (ROADMAP I26, 026ef73, from jimdawdy-hub/pg_fts e4bba08);
- a change to how fuzz property 8 is timed (3ae7788).

Not in it: main's I19b readings change (1b3ca35), the `fts_vacuum` tombstone rewrite (bba830f) and the per-backend tombstone memo.  They wait for the next release.
