# pg_fts 1.15.1 release record (2026-10-29)

**Current.**  1.15.1 fixes ROADMAP I9a: 1.15.0's chunked re-parse of documents over 16,383
positions split HTML markup at its chunk boundaries.  I found it by comparing 1.15.0's output
with an unchunked parser.  Scripts and logs are in `data_150_2026-10-29/i9cmp/`.

## 1. The exact reference

The reference is the downstream `jimdawdy-hub/pg_fts` branch `upgrade/freecase-130-20261015`
(a0b5355, pg_fts 1.13.0 with `vendor/ts_parse_wide.c`).  That file is PostgreSQL's `parsetext`
vendored with a 30-bit position counter and no 16,383 clamp, so long text is parsed in one pass
with no chunks.  It is exact for long documents, but it is not an option for pg_fts itself:
`parsetext` changed in 18.6 (a per-lexeme length check, e251350573e), so a copy drifts from
the server.

## 2. Probes (PG 17.10, a UTF8 database with C.UTF-8 ctype, EC2 c6id.4xlarge, Debian 13)

16 long documents x `simple` / `english`.  Each probe compares
`md5(to_ftsdoc(cfg, t)::text)` and `ftsdoc_length`:
- `docs` (i9cmp.sh), 10 documents: plain; HTML tags every 200 B; comments; URLs and e-mail;
  hyphenation; CJK with no spaces and spaced; script/style; numbers; long words.  Several of
  these are under 16,383 positions and take the whole-parse path.
- `docs2` (i9cmp2.sh), 4 markup documents over 16,383 positions: tags, comments,
  script/style, mixed.
- `docs3` (i9cmp6.sh), 2 documents with markup longer than a chunk: a 40 kB `<script>` body,
  and a 30 kB `data:` URI inside a tag.

is parsed once, as in 1.15.0 and 1.14.3.

## 3. Reviews (`data_150_2026-10-29/review/review3_*`, `review4_*`, verbatim)

**Review of 7b70034** (no blocker) raised:
- markup longer than the look-ahead was still split;
- the token list could exceed MaxAllocSize where 1.15.0 did not;
- the pointer check was formed before validation;
- this record was missing.

All were fixed in 057fcc5.  The position-bounded chunks came out of its item 6.

**Re-review of 057fcc5** (no blocker) raised:
- the "rest fits" shortcut could fire after a fallback cut that started inside a token;
- the fallback ignored the retry budget;
- two test probes were weak.

These were fixed in 803f09a.
