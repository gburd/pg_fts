# ROADMAP I23 and the I19b readings skip (2026-11-01)

**Current.**  Two questions from Codeberg #6 (robinlahtinen, 2026-10-30), measured on one
EC2 host.  Scripts and raw logs are in `data_i23_2026-11-01/`.

1. Is a pure OR slower on 1.15.x than on 1.14.3?  The reporter measured
   `build | (10 terms)` at 519-547 ms on 1.14.3 and 557-582 ms on 1.15.1.
2. What does 1b3ca35 (the collect path skips a segment when an AND operand of plain
   readings is all absent) buy on the reporter's `(zzzq | zzzx) & kur:*` shape?

**Answers:**

1. **Not reproduced.**  A pure OR is within the noise of 1.14.3 on two corpora, both PG
   majors, at both scales.
2. **About 50x on the reporter's corpus and 8,000x on a larger index**, with identical results.

## Method

- **Host:** c6id.metal (Xeon Platinum 8375C, 2 NUMA nodes), Debian 13, kernel 6.12.
  - Turbo off (`intel_pstate/no_turbo = 1`), `performance` governor, THP `never`.
  - **Pinned:** the postmaster and its backends on cores 8-15 of node 0, psql on 16-17,
    memory on node 0.
  - Not controlled: C-states, SMT; no hardware counters were read.
- **Software:** PostgreSQL 17.10 and 18.6 built from source with `-O2`, Debian's gcc 14.
- **Builds:** four `.so` files per PG major, compiled with the same compiler and flags:
  - `v1143`: 1.14.3, 10a03ac;
  - `v1151`: 1.15.1, 551b13a;
  - `head`: 1b3ca35;
  - `head2`: 1b3ca35 again, a second build of the same tree used as an A/A control.
- **One index, swapped libraries.**  Each cluster has one index, built once.  Between
  measurements the server is stopped, the `.so` is replaced, and the server is started
  again.  The SQL objects and the on-disk format are identical from 1.14.3 to 1.15.1, and
  this is what the reporter did.
- **Correctness before timing.**  For every build, the top-10 ids are equal across builds
  and to `pg_fts.bestfirst = off`, and the heap match count is recorded.  There were no
  mismatches.
- **Timing:**
  - Each sample is the median of 21 executions after 4 warm-ups (11 after 3 on the 640k
    corpus), taken in one psql session with `\timing`.
  - Rounds run the builds in ABBA order, alternating direction each round, with 5 s
    between runs.
  - 6 rounds give 12 samples per build.
  - The figures below are the median of those samples, with the min-max in brackets.

## Corpora

- **400k:** the reporter's corpus from #6, deterministic with `setseed(0.6)`.  Each row has
  4 filler words `w0..w2000`; `apple` and `banana` are each in about 25% of rows; `build`
  is in 84 rows with none of the OR terms.  `i23.sh` (PG 17) and `i23m.sh` (PG 18).
- **640k:** a stand-in for the reporter's 640k-row corpus of longer documents.  Each row
  has 60 tokens drawn log-uniformly over `t1..t5000` (heavy-tailed), and `build` is in 84
  rows.  `i23b.sh` and `i23c.sh`, PG 18.  On this corpus a 10-term OR costs 41-98 ms,
  the regime where the walk dominates.

## 1. Pure OR: no regression

All figures are ms.

| query | corpus | v1143 | v1151 | head | head2 (A/A) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `build \| apple \| banana \| w1..w8` | 400k, PG 17 | 1.177 [1.172-1.188] | 1.178 [1.173-1.187] | 1.181 [1.172-1.273] | 1.179 [1.175-1.189] |
| same | 400k, PG 18 | 1.188 [1.178-1.191] | 1.191 [1.185-1.202] | 1.195 [1.192-1.210] | 1.198 [1.190-1.206] |
| `build \| t10..t19` (636,051 matches) | 640k, PG 18 | 97.958 [97.523-98.925] | 97.514 [97.253-98.671] | 97.773 [97.611-98.982] | 97.790 [97.545-98.827] |
| `build \| t40..t58` (491,444 matches) | 640k, PG 18 | 41.112 [40.960-41.376] | 41.227 [41.149-41.613] | 41.480 [41.342-41.532] | 41.474 [41.419-42.446] |

- **v1151 against v1143, change in the median:** +0.04%, +0.25%, -0.45% and +0.28%.
- **A/A, head2 against head:** between -0.21% and +0.21% on the same rows (-0.21%, +0.21%, +0.02%, -0.01%).

**Verdict: STABLE, no effect.**  Every between-build difference is under 0.5%, about the
size of the A/A spread and an order of magnitude below the reported 5-8%.

The largest difference between versions is head vs 1.14.3 on `build | t40..t58`, +0.89%
(head vs 1.14.3 on the 400k corpus: +0.34% on PG 17 and +0.59% on PG 18).  The ranges
barely overlap there (41.342 vs 41.376), so it may be real.  If so, it is under 1% and
comes from code layout, since this path has no new code.

Index buffers for the 400k OR are 1,779 on both 1.14.3 and head.

The reporter's 5-8% is not reproduced on this host, these compilers or these corpora.
Their figures include their application's access filtering, and their corpus and
documents differ from ours.  They also swapped the `.so` the same way, so the swap itself
is ruled out as the cause here, not there.  I23 stays open as "not reproduced"; the next
step needs the reporter's query plan or corpus statistics.

## 2. The readings skip (1b3ca35, ROADMAP I19b)

All figures are ms.

| query | corpus | v1151 | head | head2 (A/A) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `(zzzq \| zzzx) & (apple \| ban:*)`, 0 rows | 400k, PG 17 | 3.792 [3.774-3.808] | **0.076** [0.076-0.079] | 0.077 |
| same | 400k, PG 18 | 3.794 [3.783-3.808] | **0.078** [0.078-0.079] | 0.078 |
| `zzzq & (apple \| ban:*)` (1.15.0's single-term skip) | 400k, PG 17 | 0.070 | 0.070 | 0.070 |
| `(zzzq \| zzzx) & (t10 \| t2:*)`, 0 rows | 640k, PG 18 | 618.269 [612.396-619.665] | **0.076** [0.075-0.077] | 0.075 |
| `(zzzq \| build) & (t10 \| t2:*)`, 84 rows (a reading present) | 640k, PG 18 | 621.276 | 621.172 | 621.336 |

- **Effect:** the readings shape now costs what the single-term form does.  On the 400k
  corpus it went from 3.79 to 0.077 ms on both majors; on the 640k corpus, from 618 to
  0.076 ms.
- **Buffers:** 400k, 64 on 1.14.3 -> 22 on head; 640k, 2,217 hit + 1,478 read on 1.15.1 -> 21 hit + 1 read on head.
- **Results unchanged:** top-10 ids are equal on every build, and the heap count is 0.
- **Control:** when one reading is present (`build`, 84 rows) the group is still built,
  and the time is unchanged at 621 ms on every build.  This is the open part of I19b: a
  rare but present required operand.

## 3. Also confirmed at a second scale (1.14.3 -> 1.15.x)

The 400k corpus on PG 18 repeats the 1.15.0 results that were measured on PG 17:

| query | v1143 | v1151 |
|---|---|---|
| `zzzq & (apple \| ban:*)` | 3.78 ms | 0.071 ms |
| `build & (apple \| banana \| w1..w8)` | 18.96 ms | 0.714 ms |

That is the I19b single-term skip and I19a, respectively.
